04 // RESEARCH

Academic Contribution

Peer-reviewed bioinformatic deep learning research addressing genomic targeting precision.

PEER-REVIEWED PUBLICATION
Journal of Advance and Future Research (JAAFR) · Vol. 4, Issue 3 · March 2026

"Deep Learning-Based Prediction of CRISPR gRNA Efficiency and Off-Target Risk Using Convolutional Neural Networks"

CRISPR-Cas9 genome editing relies crucially on selecting single guide RNAs (gRNAs) that achieve high on-target cleavage efficiency while minimizing unintended off-target double-strand breaks. This research develops a specialized deep Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) architecture trained on genomic sequence matrices to simultaneously evaluate guide efficiency and score off-target mismatch propensity.

Sequence Representation

One-hot encoding of 20-nt target sequences and flanking PAM sites, preserving spatial nucleotide arrangements for convolution filters.

CNN Feature Extraction

Multi-filter convolution layers extracting GC-content motifs, position-dependent nucleotide weights, and mismatch positional penalties.

Off-Target Risk Scoring

Quantitative scoring algorithm evaluating sequence similarity thresholds and non-specific binding probabilities across potential genomic loci.
